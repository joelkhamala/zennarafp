ZENNARA manages residential and commercial properties with disciplined reporting, proactive maintenance, and transparent financial administration. Our institutional approach ensures your investment performs optimally.

What We Do

Comprehensive Management Services

From tenant placement to financial reporting, we handle every aspect of property operations.

Tenant Placement & Screening

Comprehensive tenant vetting including credit checks, employment verification, reference validation, and background screening. We identify qualified, reliable tenants who protect your property value.

  • Credit & financial assessment
  • Employment & income verification
  • Landlord reference checks
  • Background & criminal screening
  • Lease negotiation & execution

Rent Collection & Arrears Management

Disciplined rent collection with automated reminders, online payment options, and professional arrears recovery. SecureRent Programme available for guaranteed landlord income.

  • Monthly rent collection & reconciliation
  • Automated payment reminders
  • Multiple payment channels
  • Arrears follow-up & recovery
  • SecureRent guaranteed payment option

Landlord Financial Reporting

Transparent, detailed monthly statements showing all income and expenses. Annual accounts, tax documentation, and real-time portal access to your property financials.

  • Monthly income & expense statements
  • Annual financial summaries
  • Tax documentation support
  • Real-time portal access
  • Budget forecasting & analysis

Lease Administration

Complete lease lifecycle management including drafting, renewals, amendments, and terminations. Compliance with local tenancy laws across all East Africa jurisdictions.

  • Lease preparation & execution
  • Renewal negotiations
  • Rent review administration
  • Lease termination management
  • Legal compliance & documentation

Property Inspections

Regular property inspections (move-in, periodic, move-out) with detailed photographic reports. Early identification of maintenance issues and tenant compliance monitoring.

  • Move-in condition reports
  • Quarterly property inspections
  • Move-out assessments
  • Photographic documentation
  • Maintenance issue identification

Maintenance Coordination

24/7 tenant maintenance requests, vetted contractor network, work order management, and quality control. Emergency response protocols ensure rapid issue resolution.

  • 24/7 maintenance request system
  • Vetted contractor network
  • Work order management
  • Quality control & follow-up
  • Emergency response protocols

Management Fee Structure

Our fee structure is simple, transparent, and aligned with your property's performance.

8-10%
of Gross Monthly Rent (GMR)

What's Included:

  • Tenant placement & screening
  • Monthly rent collection
  • Landlord financial reporting
  • Lease administration
  • Property inspections (quarterly)
  • Maintenance coordination

  • Client portal access
  • Legal compliance management

Why Our Fee Structure Works

✓

Performance Aligned

We only earn when you earn. Our fee is calculated on collected rent, incentivizing us to maintain high occupancy and minimize arrears.

✓

No Hidden Costs

No setup fees, no monthly minimums, no surprise charges. The management fee covers all standard services listed above.

✓

Transparent Pass-Through

Maintenance and contractor costs are passed through at actual cost with no markup. You see exactly what you're paying for.

The Process

How SecureRent Works

A simple three-step process that guarantees your rental income every month.

1

Rent Falls Due

Tenant rent becomes due at the start of each month according to the lease agreement.

2

You Get Paid

By the 15th of the month, ZENNARA pays you in full — regardless of whether the tenant has paid or not.

3

We Collect

ZENNARA recovers the outstanding rent from the tenant, plus applicable late fees as per the lease terms.

Common Questions

SecureRent FAQ

What properties are eligible for SecureRent?

+
SecureRent is available for residential and commercial properties under ZENNARA management. Properties must have active leases with rent collection managed by ZENNARA. Enrollment is subject to property condition assessment and lease term review.

Is there an additional fee for SecureRent?

+
SecureRent is included as part of our comprehensive property management service. Our standard management fee (8-10% of Gross Monthly Rent) covers SecureRent protection, rent collection, tenant management, and financial reporting.

What happens if a tenant never pays?

+
You continue receiving your guaranteed payment by the 15th. ZENNARA pursues all available remedies including late fees, formal notices, and lease termination if necessary. Any amounts recovered from the tenant (including late fees) are reconciled monthly. Persistent non-payment results in lease enforcement and tenant replacement.

Can I enroll existing tenancies?

+
Yes. Properties with existing tenants can be enrolled in SecureRent when management is transferred to ZENNARA. We review the current lease terms, tenant payment history, and property condition before enrollment. Properties with problematic payment histories may require additional security measures.

How do late fees work?

+
Late fees are applied according to the lease agreement terms when tenants pay after the due date. These fees are collected from the tenant along with the outstanding rent. Late fee revenue offsets the timing risk ZENNARA assumes by paying you before tenant payment is received.

What reporting do I receive?

+
You receive detailed monthly statements by the 18th showing your guaranteed payment, actual tenant payment status, any late fees collected, and running account balances. All reporting is accessible through the ZENNARA Client Portal with real-time updates and historical records.

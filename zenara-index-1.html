SecureRent Programme

Guaranteed rent. Every month. On time.

Under SecureRent, enrolled landlords receive their rental income by the 15th of every month — whether or not the tenant has paid. ZENNARA fronts the payment and recovers the amount from the tenant together with a late fee.

This is certainty of income, professionally managed. Your property generates reliable cash flow while we handle tenant relations, collections, and enforcement.

Learn More About SecureRent
Step 1
Tenant rent falls due at the start of the month.
Step 2
By the 15th, the landlord is paid in full by ZENNARA — regardless of tenant payment status.
Step 3
ZENNARA recovers the outstanding rent from the tenant, plus the applicable late fee.

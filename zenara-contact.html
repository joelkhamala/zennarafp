Frequently Asked Questions

What areas do you serve?

ZENNARA operates across Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, and Rwanda, with primary offices in major cities including Nairobi, Kampala, Dar es Salaam, and Kigali.

What is your management fee structure?

Our standard property management fee is 8-10% of Gross Monthly Rent (GMR). SecureRent Programme has additional premiums based on risk assessment. Contact us for a detailed proposal.

How quickly can I get started?

After initial consultation and property assessment, we can typically onboard your property within 2-3 weeks, depending on current tenant status and documentation.

Do you handle property sales or brokerage?

No. ZENNARA focuses exclusively on property and facility management services. We do not engage in property sales, brokerage, or lettings transactions.

ZENNARA LTD delivers institutional-grade property and facility management services across Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, and Rwanda. Our disciplined approach ensures reliable income, transparent reporting, and proactive asset maintenance.

Who We Are

Institutional Property Management

ZENNARA operates as a professional property and facility management firm focused exclusively on managing assets—not selling them. This singular focus eliminates conflicts of interest and ensures our clients' properties receive the dedicated attention they deserve.

Our approach is disciplined, data-driven, and transparent. We provide monthly financial statements, maintain rigorous preventive maintenance schedules, and offer guaranteed rental income through our SecureRent Programme.

Operating across four East African nations, ZENNARA serves residential and commercial property owners who value professional management, reliable cash flow, and peace of mind.

The ZENNARA Difference

01

Management Only, No Conflicts

Unlike firms that handle both property sales and management, ZENNARA focuses exclusively on managing assets. We have no incentive to sell your property—only to maximize its performance.

SecureRent Guarantee

Our flagship SecureRent Programme guarantees landlord payment by the 15th of every month, regardless of tenant payment status. Financial certainty for property owners.

03

Disciplined Reporting

Monthly financial statements delivered by the 5th of each month. Annual accounts, real-time portal access, and transparent documentation of every transaction.

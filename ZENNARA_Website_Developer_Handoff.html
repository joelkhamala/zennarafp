Usage Rule — Gold

Gold marks business metrics, calls to action, section labels, and premium emphasis. Use sparingly against charcoal; never as a large fill.

Greens are reserved for the Z-Prism mark, the ZEN F&P line, SecureRent, and sustainability content. Business copy stays gold/off-white.

05 · Flagship Product

The SecureRent Programme

This is ZENNARA's core commercial differentiator and must be the most prominent product story on the site — featured on the homepage and given its own page.

Guaranteed rent. Every month. On time.

Under SecureRent, enrolled landlords receive their rental income by the 15th of every month — whether or not the tenant has paid. ZENNARA fronts the payment and recovers the amount from the tenant together with a late fee.

Position it as certainty of income, professionally managed. The late-fee recovery is an internal revenue mechanic — reference it factually, never as the headline.

Step 1Tenant rent falls due at the start of the month.
Step 2By the 15th, the landlord is paid in full by ZENNARA — regardless of tenant payment status.
Step 3ZENNARA recovers the outstanding rent from the tenant, plus the applicable late fee.
